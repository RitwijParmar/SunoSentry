# Natural voice demo

The demo now uses the browser's installed English voice catalog rather than a
fixed robotic-sounding default. It prefers `Samantha`, `Ava`, `Karen`, Google
US English, Microsoft Aria, or Daniel when available, then applies a measured
rate and pitch for conversational delivery. The user can change the voice in
the selector and press **Play natural voice demo**.

Demo narration:

> Welcome to SunoSentry. I listen for the service issue, check the approved
> safety policy, and ask for explicit confirmation before sending a proposal to
> a human dispatcher. If the request is unsafe or unclear, I stop and escalate.

This is browser speech synthesis, not a recorded customer call. Audio is not
uploaded, persisted, or used as training data. Voice availability and quality
depend on the browser and operating system running the demo.
